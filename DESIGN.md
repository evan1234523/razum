## Brand & Style

This design system fuses raw vehicular combat grit with the electrifying typographic rebellion of early-2000s Y2K underground racing graphics. It strips away sterile aerospace/military tropes in favor of demolition derby engine bays, hot-rod garage tuning, scraped chrome badges, and analog cathode-ray CRT diagnostics.

### Aesthetic Influences & Visual Direction
- **Y2K Street Racing & Demolition Culture:** Heavy forward-slanting italic letterforms, planetary wireframe insignias, halftoned tournament badges, chrome-sheen emblems, and Japanese drift-culture katakana labels (`ドメイン`, `レーシング`).
- **Cockpit Mechanical Tactility (FUMES Aesthetic):** Corroded steel instrument cluster mounts, exposed zip-ties and braided wiring harnesses, tactile toggle switches, stamped serial plates, and analog needle-sweep gauges with phosphor illumination.
- **Gritty Contrast & Print Distress:** Screen-printed sticker layers, stencil-cut part numbers, worn paint transfer on carbon steel, and rough bitmap dithering overlaying pure black base plates.

### Target Audience & Emotional Impact
Aimed at underground drivers, combat mechanics, and gearheads who relish the stench of burnt rubber, oil slicks, screeching superchargers, and high-stakes survival. The interface feels visceral, heavy, tactile, and dangerously mechanical.

## Colors

The palette establishes an aggressive, grease-stained hierarchy. Deep charcoal and slate plates anchor the system, ensuring high-contrast legibility for bright chrome, stencil marks, and functional indicators.

### Palette Architecture
- **Base Surfaces:**
  - `void-black` (`#0B0C0E`): Cockpit baseline, deep CRT display backing, and screen borders.
  - `charcoal-plate` (`#16181D`): Primary housing, gauge wells, bolt-down chassis plates.
  - `industrial-slate` (`#242831`): Recessed component bays, card containers, sliders tracks, and inactive buttons.
- **High-Contrast Marks & Text:**
  - `signal-off-white` (`#F4F4F6`): Stencil cuts, primary titles, critical speed readouts, and decal lettering.
  - `racing-silver` / `chrome-sheen` (`#E2E8F0` / `#CBD5E1`): Metallic secondary telemetry, borders, and Y2K metallic bevels.
- **Controlled Functional Accents (Strictly Functional):**
  - `overheat-crimson` (`#EF4444`): RPM redline warnings, critical hull breach, ammo exhaustion, and primary trigger locks.
  - `phosphor-amber` (`#FF9D00`): Gear shifts, boost thresholds, fuel low, and active selected tuning parameters.
  - `crt-green` (`#22C55E`): Terminal boot lines, nominal radiator temperature, weapon readiness, and low-res auxiliary camera feeds.

## Typography

The typography unites aggressive Y2K street display forms with raw analog terminal readouts.

### Hierarchy & Style Execution
- **Display Headlines (Anybody):** Rendered ultra-bold (800–900 weight), frequently styled with a forced forward slant (`font-style: italic`), tight tracking, and uppercase transform. Emulates early-2000s tuning logos, chrome vehicle emblems, and demolition tournament banners.
- **Cockpit Telemetry & Data Readouts (Space Mono):** Fixed-width monospace typography drives all live gauges, digital speed readouts, coordinate grids, and equipment parameters. Tabular spacing prevents layout twitching under active frame updates.
- **Industrial Part Numbers & Micro-Labels (Barlow Condensed):** Condensed, robust, and uppercase. Stretched tracking (+0.08em to +0.12em) captures stamped chassis tags, engine block serial numbers, and Japanese street race badges.
- **Katakana & Stencil Badges:** Secondary decal badges pair bold Western type with Japanese characters (`ニトロ [NITRO]`, `過給器 [BLOWER]`, `最高速 [TOP SPEED]`) to reinforce underground tuning pedigree.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built as a dense, perimeter-focused cockpit dashboard with physical pod mounts, modular bolt-on brackets, and peripheral instrumentation.

### Layout Principles
- **Cockpit HUD Clustered Perimeter:** Central viewport remains clear for roadway target tracking. Instrumentation clusters clamp to the lower left (tire heat, armor integrity, boost reserves) and lower right (speed, tachometer, gear readout, heat gauge).
- **Subsystem Rack Grid:** Tuning and garage menus use a tight 12-column modular grid with 16px gutters. Pods fit together with zero superfluous negative space, replicating real-world instrument racks held together by mounting frames.
- **Breakpoints & Adaptation:**
  - **Desktop / Ultrawide (>1024px):** Gauges expand into dual lower-wing consoles with auxiliary CRT terminal feeds anchored to the upper frame. 20px outer margin.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** Bottom consoles compress into a unified horizontal dashboard strip. Micro-readouts collapse into segmented status bars.
  - **Mobile (<767px):** Critical speed and gear telemetry convert to a top/bottom HUD visor with large 22px tabular readouts and simplified toggle strips. 16px outer margin.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is expressed strictly through bolted physical metal enclosures, inset instrument wells, and cathode-ray glow—completely avoiding sterile software blurs or soft ambient shadows.

### Mechanical Stacking Model
- **Base Level (Steel Bulkhead):** Solid `#0B0C0E` chassis background with stamped texture and simulated sheet-metal seamlines.
- **Recessed Wells (Gauges & Sliders):** Recessed 2px into panels using a hard 1px top/left shadow (`#000000`) and a crisp 1px bottom/right metallic edge (`#242831`).
- **Raised Hardware Plates:** Instrument pods sit 2px above the bulkhead with hard 1px `#242831` borders, angled 45-degree chamfers, and simulated hex-screw caps at corner junctions.
- **CRT Glass Lens & Phosphor Tube Glow:** Displays utilize a 1px scanline mask. Active illuminated states (such as Overheat Crimson or Phosphor Amber indicators) emit an intense, tight 2px to 4px hard-edged optical glow with zero ambient diffusion.

## Shapes

The interface embraces a strictly sharp, angular geometry (`roundedness: 0`), reflecting welded steel armor and sheet-metal bodywork.

### Geometric Rules
- **0px Border Radius:** All frames, data cells, badges, and buttons maintain crisp, sharp 90-degree right angles.
- **45-Degree Chamfer Corners:** Primary containers, weapon slots, and tournament badges utilize 8px to 12px cut corners (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) to mimic sheared armor plate and heavy machinery gussets.
- **Analog Dial Radii:** Curves are strictly reserved for functional tachometer dials, circular boost gauges, and Y2K planetary wireframe badges.

## Components

### Buttons & Ignition Triggers
- **Primary Engage / Ignition Button:** 0px sharp block button in `#EF4444` (Overheat Crimson) or raw `#F4F4F6` with deep black uppercase italic typography (**Anybody** 16px). Features an angled top-right chamfer cut. Active state causes an immediate 2px inset displacement with a brief white phosphor flash.
- **Mechanical Toggle & Pod Action:** Dark `#16181D` button bounded by a 1px `#242831` border. Hover triggers a solid `#E2E8F0` hairline border with a faint green or amber status LED pip lighting up in the corner.

### Gauges & Dial Clusters
- **Scrap Tachometer / Speedometer:** Heavy bolted circular bezel housing an analog swept needle in `#EF4444`. Index tick lines in `#F4F4F6` with critical redline band starting at 7,000 RPM. Centered inside the dial is a large tabular digital readout in `Space Mono`.
- **Vertical Segmented LED Bars:** Multi-segment fuel, heat, and armor bars. Composed of discrete rectangular blocks separated by 2px gaps. Normal operational levels glow in `#22C55E` or `#E2E8F0`; warning thresholds switch instantly to `#FF9D00`, redlining into flashing `#EF4444`.

### Chips, Part Numbers & Tournament Badges
- **Y2K Racing Badges:** Chrome-bordered pills or starburst-crested oval badges combining heavy italic headers, wireframe globes, and Japanese katakana sub-labels.
- **Component Identifier Tags:** Stenciled badges (`[TURBO_01: STG-3]`, `[ARMOR: SLAG_WELD]`) rendered in `#F4F4F6` text inside `#242831` containers with corner crop marks (`┌ ┐`).

### Inputs & Mechanical Sliders
- **Analog Tuning Sliders:** 4px recessed channel in `#0B0C0E` bordered by `#242831`. Slider thumb is an extruded rectangular silver/amber slider block with knurled grip lines and an active digital value box (`0.00` to `1.00`) docked directly to the right.
- **Checkboxes & Toggles:** Square 14px metal housings with 0px radius. Checked state features a high-visibility diagonal slash or solid phosphor square fill.

### Cards, Tooling Pods & CRT Terminals
- **Equipment Slot Cards:** Stamped steel pods in `#16181D` with 1px `#242831` borders. Cards feature top technical label bars (`SYS_CFG // DEV_04`), component wireframes, and active stat counters.
- **CRT Terminal Dialogue Pod:** Inset display simulating an in-cockpit green-phosphor CRT screen. Features scanlines, bracketed boundary borders, bitmap dither accents, and dialogue text scrolling in `Space Mono` green (`#22C55E`) on `#0B0C0E`.
